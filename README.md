## 🧭 Why AI?

During my bachelor's, the subjects I enjoyed the most were the ones about **algorithmic decision making**: turning a real problem into a model a machine can reason about and solve.

That's why I specialized with a **Master's in Artificial Intelligence**. I believe AI is the most promising paradigm to improve the decisions we make every day and to help society run more efficiently. 🌍
